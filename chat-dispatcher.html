Before a chat advances

  1. No Stop, thinking, streaming, or pending submission.
  2. The latest reply is complete and stable for 1.5 seconds.
  3. The chat confirms the task’s PR URL and merge commit.
  4. GitHub independently confirms that exact merge.
  5. After three verified jobs, the idle old chat is detached and closed, and a new chat takes its slot.

Text drafts stay untouched unless replacement is explicitly enabled while paused. Attachments and selected context are always protected. Closed tabs and uncertain sends stop their slot for review.
